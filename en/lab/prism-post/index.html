How do I deliver light?

Tap mirrors to switch between / and backslash. Route every colored source on the left to its matching mailbox on the right at the same time. Undo freely and keep adjusting loops or escaped beams; there is no timer. The first three routes introduce coral letters, then teal joins in. Hints correct one mirror from a reference solution and cap the result at two stars. Routes unlock in order.

This is a discrete grid-reflection puzzle. The prism represents already-separated colors; it does not simulate physical refraction or optics. Progress stays in this browser.
