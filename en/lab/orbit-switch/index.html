How to play

Rings are numbered from the center outward. Each ring has a moving light and a fixed star. Use its left and right buttons to rotate it. From level 11, turning a ring also turns its outer neighbor in the opposite direction. The outermost ring has no linked neighbor. Align every light to win. Undo, restart, and hints are available. Progress stays on this device.
