How do I dispatch trains?

Each train moves one cell per beat along its colored route and cannot stop midway. It waits off-track before departure and leaves after arrival. Departure 0 means start immediately.

Two trains sharing a cell on the same beat or swapping adjacent cells head-on cause a collision. All trains must arrive by the deadline. Step through your plan, then edit the schedule to try again.

A discrete route-and-timing puzzle, not a real railway or physics simulation. Explore 150 shifts in six chapters; the first three teach the controls.
