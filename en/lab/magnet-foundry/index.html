How does the magnetic field work?

The core is always north-pole. Click a side magnet: S pulls the core toward it; N pushes the core away. The core slides until a barrier or edge stops it. After a successful move that magnet swaps polarity. Stop exactly on the gold target; passing over it does not count. Blocked pulses do not cost a move or swap polarity.

Check the button arrows, plan where barriers will stop the core, then anticipate the next polarity swap. W/D/S/A activate the top/right/bottom/left magnets. Undo and hints affect your stars.
