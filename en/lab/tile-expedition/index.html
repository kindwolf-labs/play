How do you travel light?

Take only the top tile of each stack. Three matching marks clear automatically from your pack. Removing a tile uncovers the one below it. The pack holds 7 tiles in the first half and 6 later; filling it blocks further moves. Undo, restart, or ask for a safe next step. A hint never takes a tile for you. Clear every tile to unlock the next stop.

Keyboard: Tab selects a tile; Enter or Space takes it. Progress is saved on this device. No purchases, ads, or account required.
