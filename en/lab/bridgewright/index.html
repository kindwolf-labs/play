How do you design a bridge network?

Click a dashed route to build a bridge; its number is the timber cost. Click again to remove it and refund the timber. Connect every island within budget. A loop usually wastes timber, so compare cheaper routes first. Undo or restart to replan. A hint marks a bridge in a minimum-cost solution and limits the result to two stars. This is a route-budget puzzle, not a structural simulation.
