How do the patterns meet?

Choose an axis and a grid line. The dashed line marks the crease. Use the arrow buttons to fold an entire side across it; all layers move together. The paper can extend beyond its old edge and is recentered automatically.

Blank cells are paper too. Match the target width, height and stamps. Blue and gold make a double stamp; identical colors stay the same. This is a discrete pattern puzzle, with no paper thickness, front/back or physical crease simulation.

Undo any fold or restart the page. Hints search for a solution within your remaining folds; if none is found, undo a step. Finish a page to unlock the next. Replay unlocked pages from the atlas.
