Privacy and local saves

These browser builds require no game account and contain no ads or in-app purchases. Progress and settings are saved on your device. This English collection adds no analytics service or tracking SDK.

What stays in your browser

Games can save level progress, unlocked stages, stars, scores, current runs, language and sound preferences using browser storage. The exact save fields vary by game. These builds do not provide account backup or automatic cross-device synchronization.

Hosting and external websites

GitHub Pages serves these pages and their files. Loading them sends network requests to the hosting service, which may process technical access information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs under its own policy. Local game saves do not mean the hosting provider has no access logs.

Read the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Feedback links open GitHub; other platforms have their own terms and data practices.

Keeping or removing progress

Local saves remain until overwritten, removed through a game's controls, cleared by you, or removed by the browser. Clearing this site's browser storage also clears game progress and preferences. Private browsing or blocked storage may prevent saves from persisting.

Feedback and privacy questions

Use our feedback guide or public issue tracker. GitHub may require its own account. Issues are public: share only the game, level, device/browser and relevant reproduction steps. Do not post passwords, identity documents, payment details or private contact information.
