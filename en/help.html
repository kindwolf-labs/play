Game guides & feedback

All three web builds are free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Progress stays on this device and browser; it does not automatically transfer between devices or websites.

Bloom Rings

  1. Choose Play, then an unlocked level on the map.
  2. Drag or tap a ring to rotate it. Shared sockets connect the overlapping rings.
  3. Gather all beads of one color into one ring to make that color bloom.
  4. Watch the move counter. Use Undo, Retry or Hint when you get stuck.
Play Bloom Rings →

Dream Draft XI

  1. Choose the daily challenge or free mode, then pick a formation.
  2. Spin for a fictional club and era. Choose one candidate and place them in the lineup.
  3. Fill all 11 positions. Consider position fit, ratings and chemistry.
  4. Simulate the league and compare the final table. A challenge code lets others play the same sequence of spins.
Play Dream Draft XI →

Perfect Season

  1. Choose PLAY for a new season or CONTINUE to resume a saved run.
  2. Draft all 10 roster positions using the wheel and candidate cards.
  3. Read the opposing defense before calling a play: RUN beats COVERAGE, SHORT beats BLITZ, and DEEP beats STACKED BOX.
  4. Finish 17 regular-season games, earn a top-eight spot and challenge three playoff rounds.

Loading, display or save trouble?

Try a refresh and use portrait orientation with default browser zoom. If you opened the link inside a messaging app, try the same link in your usual browser. Keep your current data when reporting save problems; clearing site data or using a private session can affect saves.

For Bloom Rings and Dream Draft XI, the English launch links set the game's language preference. You can also use the in-game language switch.
