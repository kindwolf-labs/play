Kindwolf Play collection: Arrow Harbor, Copper Workshop, Garden Current, Lantern Links and Pocket Blocks.

English

These game builds require no game account and contain no advertising, payments or remote gameplay analytics. Progress and the collection’s test log stay in your browser unless you choose to export or share them. Hosting providers and external websites have their own data practices.

What stays on your device

The games use browser storage for progress, unlocked levels, stars, best scores or times, language and sound preferences. Games that support continuing a session also save the current board, pieces, moves, undo history and elapsed foreground time. A temporary run identifier distinguishes sessions; it is not an account. Storage availability depends on your browser and its privacy settings.

The collection’s local test log

When you open games inside this collection, it can record game openings, loads, starts, completions and endings, with timestamps, a temporary session identifier, the referring channel category and available level or score information. The collection keeps up to the latest 500 entries in browser storage. This is a local device log, not a central player database or remote analytics service. Opening a standalone game does not automatically upload gameplay events.

The collection offers export and clear controls. Export downloads a JSON file to your device; it does not send that file to us. If you choose to share an export or a screenshot, review it before posting.

Hosting and external links

The public collection is hosted on GitHub Pages. Delivering a web page involves requests to the hosting platform, which may process technical access information such as an IP address, browser information and request details under its own policy. Our statement that the game does not send remote gameplay analytics does not mean the hosting platform records no visits.

Read the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Links to GitHub feedback or other external sites take you to services with their own policies. A game embedded or distributed on another platform may also be subject to that platform’s policies.

Your controls and retention

Game data remains in browser storage until it is overwritten, removed by the game’s available controls, cleared by you or removed by your browser. The collection’s “clear” control removes its local event log; it does not erase every game’s progress. To remove all local game data, clear this site’s storage in your browser settings. That also removes saved progress and preferences. Private browsing, blocked storage or clearing site data can prevent a game from resuming. No cross-device or account-based backup is provided by these builds.

Feedback and privacy requests

Use kindwolf-labs/play Issues for gameplay problems, questions or a privacy-related request. GitHub may require its own account to submit an issue. Issues are public: include the game name, level, device/browser and reproduction steps, but do not post passwords, identity documents, payment information or private contact details. We do not ask for such information to play or investigate an ordinary game bug.

中文

这些游戏版本无需注册游戏账号，没有广告、支付或远端玩法统计。游戏进度和合集测试日志保存在你的浏览器内，除非你主动导出或分享。网页托管平台和外部网站有各自的数据处理规则。

本设备保存的内容

游戏会使用浏览器存储保存关卡进度、解锁状态、星级、最高分或最佳用时、语言和声音设置。支持继续上局的游戏还会保存当前棋盘、候选方块、操作次数、撤销记录和前台累计时间。临时局标识用于区分游玩过程，不是账号。是否能够持久保存取决于浏览器及隐私设置。

合集的本地测试日志

在合集内打开游戏时，合集可以记录打开、加载、开局、完成和结束事件，以及时间、临时会话标识、来源渠道类别、可用的关卡或分数信息。浏览器内最多保留最近500条。这是当前设备的本地记录，不是集中玩家数据库，也没有自行传到远端统计服务。单独打开游戏不会自动上传玩法事件。

合集提供导出和清除功能。导出会把JSON文件下载到你的设备，不会把文件发送给我们。若你主动分享导出文件或截图，请在发布前检查内容。

托管平台与外部链接

公开合集由GitHub Pages托管。加载网页需要向托管平台发送网络请求；平台可能依据自身政策处理IP地址、浏览器信息、请求详情等技术访问记录。“游戏没有远端玩法统计”不表示托管平台完全不记录访问。

请参阅GitHub通用隐私声明。前往GitHub反馈或其他外部网站时，其服务适用各自政策。在其他平台嵌入或分发的游戏，也可能受该平台政策约束。

保存期限和清除方式

本地数据会保留到被覆盖、通过游戏提供的功能删除、由你清除或被浏览器移除。合集的“清除”只删除合集事件日志，不会清掉所有游戏进度。若需删除全部本地游戏数据，可在浏览器设置中清除本网站存储；这也会删除已保存的进度和偏好。无痕模式、禁用存储或清除网站数据可能导致无法续玩。这些版本没有账号备份或跨设备同步。

问题反馈与隐私请求

请通过kindwolf-labs/play Issues提交游戏问题、疑问或隐私相关请求。GitHub可能要求登录其自身账号。Issue内容公开可见：建议提供游戏名、关卡、设备／浏览器和复现步骤，不要填写密码、身份证件、支付信息或私人联系方式。游玩及普通故障排查不需要这些信息。
