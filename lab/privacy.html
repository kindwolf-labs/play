Privacy / 隐私说明

Updated 2026-11-18 / 更新日期

隐私、进度与匿名统计

独立网页游戏无需注册游戏账号，没有广告、内购。进度保存在当前浏览器。本站使用Umami Cloud了解页面访问、实际开局、核心完成及前台有效时长。

保存在本设备的内容

游戏使用浏览器存储保存进度、解锁、星级、分数、当前局、语言与音效偏好；不会把完整存档发送给Umami。存档保留至被覆盖、由你清除或被浏览器移除。清除网站存储也会删除游戏进度；当前版本没有账号备份或跨设备同步。旧合集的本地测试日志及导出仍留在本设备，除非你主动分享。

集中统计内容与口径

仅在kindwolf-labs.github.io/play独立站发送页面路径、游戏和版本、限定的来源类别、关卡、玩法事件与前台有效时长。不发送URL查询参数与片段、账户名称、邮箱、完整存档或游戏局标识。TapTap、CrazyGames和itch.io游戏包不包含本独立站统计接入。

Umami会处理IP地址和浏览器信息，以计算近似地区及匿名会话标识。默认标识随盐值轮换，不自动跨月合并；它不等于准确自然人人数。IP不是标准分析记录字段，但服务运营、安全和基础设施日志仍可能处理IP等访问资料。本站不另设跨站或跨月追踪玩家的身份标识。

当前免费Hobby账户的数据区域为美国Oregon，分析记录保留6个月；这不表示全部基础设施或支持处理只在该区域。备份及运营日志依服务商政策处理。详见Umami隐私政策与数据处理协议。

关闭统计及反馈

下方按钮通过本浏览器的本地偏好关闭本站后续统计，不影响存档，也不会自动删除已有服务器记录；若浏览器提供Do Not Track信号，本站会尊重。开发环境、QA参数及平台预览不纳入统计；显式接入验收单独标记，不能当真实玩家。

GitHub Pages托管平台可能依据自身隐私政策处理技术访问记录。问题及隐私请求请通过公开问题反馈提交；内容公开可见，请勿填写密码、证件、支付或私人联系方式。

Privacy and local saves

These independent browser games have no game accounts, ads or purchases. Progress stays in your browser. We use Umami Cloud to understand visits, real game starts, core completions and active play time on this site.

Game data on this device

Games can store progress, unlocked levels, stars, scores, current runs, language and sound settings. Local saves are not sent to Umami. They remain until overwritten, deleted by you or removed by the browser. Clearing site storage also removes game saves. These builds do not provide account backup or cross-device synchronization.

Anonymous analytics

On kindwolf-labs.github.io/play, Umami receives a page path, game and build version, allowlisted source category, level number, gameplay event and foreground active time. URL query strings and fragments, account names, emails, full saves and game-session identifiers are not sent by our integration. TapTap, CrazyGames and itch.io game packages do not contain this site's analytics integration.

Umami also processes IP addresses and browser information to derive approximate location and an anonymous session identifier. The default identifier uses a rotating salt and is not automatically linked across monthly rotations; it is not an exact person count. IP addresses are not a standard analytics record field, but Umami and its providers may process or log technical access data for service operation and security. We do not use custom identifiers to join players across sites or months.

Our free Hobby account stores analytics in the US (Oregon), with a six-month analytics retention period. This region does not mean all infrastructure or support processing happens there. Backup and operational retention follow Umami's policies. See Umami Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

Your analytics choice

The control below disables this site's analytics in this browser using a local preference; it does not delete game progress or existing server records. We also honor an available Do Not Track signal. Local development, QA parameters and platform previews are excluded. Explicit integration tests are labelled separately and do not count as real-player samples.

Hosting, feedback and requests

GitHub Pages serves the site and may process technical access records under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Other websites have their own policies. For feedback or privacy questions, use our public issue tracker. GitHub may require its own account. Do not post passwords, identity documents, payment details or private contact information.
