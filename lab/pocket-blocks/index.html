How to play

Choose one of three shapes, then tap the board to place its top-left corner. Fill an entire row or column to grow a bloom and clear it. Use all three shapes for a fresh set. Consecutive clearing moves earn bonus points. The garden ends when no remaining shape fits. Keyboard: 1–3 to select, arrow keys to move on the board, Enter to place.
