How to play Voxel Bloom

Drag the 3D crystal to find an exposed face. Select its matching charge, then tap to blast the connected cluster. Blocks above the gap fall, so check for bigger chains after each shot.

晶簇爆破怎么玩

拖动晶体寻找露出的表面。选中对应颜色并点按方块，可以清掉相连的同色晶簇。上方的方块会下沉，每次爆破后都可以找新的连锁。
